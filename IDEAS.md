# Feature ideas

Implemented ideas are documented in the owning feature guides:

- [Disco party lights in Zippo](zippo.lib/README.md): independent color, actuation and beam-spread cycles on one light or a craft.
- [Graffiti spray mode](graffiti.lib/README.md): hold to place decals at a configurable interval.
- [Humble Arteest cursor painting](humble-arteest.lib/README.md): individual mesh instances, whole subparts or shared meshes.
